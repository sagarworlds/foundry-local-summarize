POLICY DOCUMENT: SEC-2026-03
TITLE: Enterprise Cloud Security, Data Sovereignty & Inference Directive
EFFECTIVE DATE: March 15, 2026
CLASSIFICATION: Confidential

1. DATA CLASSIFICATION & EGRESS PROHIBITION
- Enterprise Strategic Data, Financial Models, and Legal Contracts are designated "Restricted Confidential".
- Restricted Confidential data shall NOT be transmitted across public third-party LLM cloud APIs unless an explicit CISO waiver is on file.
- Local Foundry inference (offline models running on localhost) is the certified standard for all internal document summarization and analysis.

2. CONTRACT LIABILITY CAP REQUIREMENTS
- Standard liability caps must not exceed 1x annual contract value (ACV). Caps exceeding 2x ACV are categorically prohibited without General Counsel written sign-off.
- Mutual indemnification for IP infringement and data protection breaches must remain non-waivable.
